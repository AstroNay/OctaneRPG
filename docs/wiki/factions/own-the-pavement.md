# Own The Pavement (OTP)

> A loud, unstable swarm that mistakes attention for respect.

**Leader:** [Flauntive](../characters/flauntive.md) (the latest of many)  
**Home turf:** swarmed meets, back alleys, side streets at night  
**Joinable?** No. You can only earn their respect.

## The name

OTP believes the street belongs to whoever takes the most space. Numbers are their identity and intimidation is their tool.

## What they're like

OTP has been in Aurora Hills longer than anyone wants to admit. They survive on volume, money and the kind of confidence that doesn't require competence. Their cars are often immaculate on the outside, but the knowledge under the hood belongs to the mechanics they pay, not the drivers. Leadership changes like seasons; the culture stays the same.

In Aurora Hills OTP isn't respected. It's **endured**: a moving blockade of ego that every serious driver eventually has to outgrow, or join.

## History

1. **Early OTP.** A normal crew that got big and hollow.
2. **Leadership carousel.** Leaders rotate through ego fights, arrests and vanishing acts.
3. **Meet floods.** They popularize swarming meets until organizers relocate.
4. **Crackdown year.** Aurora Hills PD ramps up targeted enforcement.
5. **TEF's rise.** OTP mocks TEF, then turns to poaching and sabotage as TEF gains respect.
6. **Absorption attempts.** When other crews collapse, OTP tries to absorb them for numbers.

## Recruitment

Anyone can join if they bring an expensive-looking car, money to burn, a willingness to do reckless things for attention or social energy.

## How they sound

They say **"we"** to sound powerful. They talk status, blame the track when they lose and pivot to excuses when cornered.

## Earning OTP's respect

Public wins, reckless stunts, crime jobs and rising to their bait. It's fast, and it costs you heat, police attention and TEF's trust.

## Biggest fear

Being irrelevant.
