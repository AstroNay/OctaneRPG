# The Elite Few (TEF)

> A low-drama family of skilled outcasts who communicate quietly, drive precisely and let consistency do the talking.

**Founders:** [Sweets](../characters/sweets.md) and [Panda](../characters/panda.md)
**Home turf:** [the docks](../world/the-docks.md), quiet late-night routes, TEF-hosted meets
**Joinable?** No. You can only earn their respect.

## The name

"Elite" isn't about ego. It means discipline, maturity and respect. "Few" is literal: TEF stays small on purpose.

## History

1. **Docks nights.** Two shy drifters start practicing late, learning tandems and building quiet trust.
2. **Name and values.** "The Elite Few" is chosen deliberately: small roster, skill-first, anti-drama.
3. **First real meet.** Strangers show up, drawn by word of mouth, good music and a calm sense of order.
4. **Formation culture.** TEF starts parking in organized formations.
5. **The Easter Egg Meet.** Cars in pastel colours like easter eggs, dressed to match and parked in formation for photos.
6. **The application wave.** A larger crew collapses and its ex-members flood TEF with applications. TEF slows down instead of speeding up.
7. **The quiet reputation.** The crew people talk about in a lower voice, not because they're scary but because they're serious.

## Values

Skill over ego · control over chaos · community over clout · accountability over excuses · loyalty over trend-hopping.

## Recruitment

Invite-only in practice, proof over pitch. Applicants are watched over time, at meets and runs and in how they treat people. Senior members vote. TEF dislikes "nomads" who bounce between crews for status.

## Style

Clean, coordinated and slightly soft around the edges: pastel accents, tasteful wraps, cherry blossoms. Music matters. The meet is part car culture, part therapy.

## What TEF likes and dislikes

| Likes | Dislikes |
|---|---|
| Clean tandems, repeatable lines, calm meets | Loud disrespect, reckless "main character" driving |
| Helping people without humiliating them | Drama, clout-chasing, performative cruelty |
| Practical upgrades and junkyard wins | Anyone who needs an audience to behave |
| Late-night dock runs | **Risking civilians** (the fastest way to lose their trust) |

## Earning TEF's respect

- Drive clean and keep meets orderly.
- Build practically.
- Never risk civilians. A reckless chase drops respect sharply and doors close quietly.

TEF's respect is slow and expensive, and it is never membership.

## Biggest fear

Becoming what they stood against: big, messy and full of noise.
