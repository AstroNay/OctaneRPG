# TEF vs OTP at a glance

| | [The Elite Few](the-elite-few.md) | [Own The Pavement](own-the-pavement.md) |
|---|---|---|
| In one line | A low-drama family of skilled outcasts | A loud, unstable swarm |
| Leaders | Sweets and Panda | Flauntive |
| Values | Skill, control, community, accountability, loyalty | Quantity, clout, money, fear |
| Roster | Small on purpose, invite-only, members vote | Open to anyone |
| Look | Clean and coordinated, pastel accents | Loud high-contrast matching decals |
| Home turf | The docks, quiet routes, TEF meets | Swarmed meets, back alleys |
| Respect from | Clean driving, orderly meets, practical builds | Public wins, stunts, crime, rising to bait |
| Cost | Slow. Reckless chases drop it sharply. | Heat, police attention, TEF's trust |
| Biggest fear | Becoming big, messy and loud | Being irrelevant |

## Respect, not membership

Each crew has a **respect meter** per player. It unlocks dialogue, event invites and better jobs, and changes how members talk to you. It never puts you in the crew. Joinable crews are always [player-made](../gameplay/crews.md).

## Both can be high, at a cost

OTP work raises heat and TEF notices. A clean streak earns TEF nods and OTP calls you soft.

*Respect tracking is planned; in the alpha you only meet both crews at the end of Chapter 0.*
