# Parts and stats

A car's performance comes from its base stats plus the parts installed on it. Parts have a condition
(for example *Usable* or *Installed*) and can be held in your inventory before they are fitted.

`/inventory` shows the parts a player holds and `/stats` shows a player's total stats.
