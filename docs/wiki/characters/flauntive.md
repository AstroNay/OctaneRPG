# Flauntive

**Crew:** [Own The Pavement](../factions/own-the-pavement.md), current leader.

## Profile

Flauntive isn't a mastermind. He's worse: confidently shallow. He learned that a loud voice and a big crew can impersonate authority, and he presents OTP as the "real" power in Aurora Hills.

- Charismatic in a cheap way. He performs constantly and treats reputation like a weapon.
- Aggressive, messy driver who relies on intimidation more than precision.
- Rewards loyalty and punishes embarrassment.
- If he loses, it was "rigged".

He is the latest in a long carousel of OTP leaders lost to ego fights and arrests.

## Weakness

He can't tolerate being ignored. TEF's refusal to react to him drives him up the wall.

## How he talks

Greasy charm and constant performance. Needs a reaction.

## Where to find him

In the back alleys at night, rarely alone.
His Chasers and Enforcers hang around with him.
