# Jun "Jumpstart" Park

**Role:** rival 1 at the Zeroyon drag strip.

An overconfident strip regular with a loud, cheap car who only picks fights with newcomers. He wants OTP to notice him; they don't.

## The nickname

It has two meanings: he **jumps the start** at the lights, and he **jumps on every new face** at the strip.

## How he races

His launches are fast, but he red-lights now and then (about one run in five). A careful player can beat a faster car by leaving on green.

## How he talks

Trash talk and nicknames for everyone. He calls you **"Rental"**. After a loss he folds into grudging respect.

## Strategy

Stage, wait for the third amber, go on green. See [Racing](../gameplay/racing.md#zeroyon-drag-strip).
