# Panda

**Crew:** [The Elite Few](../factions/the-elite-few.md), co-founder and the organizer.

## Profile

Down-to-earth and big-hearted, with a systems brain that still feels human. Slightly scattered (ADHD) and kind with it.

- Grew up in a military household and early PC culture.
- Struggled with introverted confidence; found belonging through cars and people who don't demand performance.
- Likes: automation and simplifying processes, animals (cats, otters, pandas), pink accents, cute anime avatars, cherry blossoms, quiet late-night drives and clean tandems.

## In TEF

Keeps the crew running smoothly so everyone can spend their time on what matters: driving, learning and being together. At meets, Panda runs the tech so the vibe stays calm.

## How they talk

Slightly scattered phrasing, always kind. Thinks in steps, exits and repeatability. Praise is practical and disapproval is reality-based, with no speeches.

## Where to find them

At the Car Meet at the [docks](../world/the-docks.md), with [Sweets](sweets.md).
