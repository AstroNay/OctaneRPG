# Sweets

**Crew:** [The Elite Few](../factions/the-elite-few.md), founder and the face of TEF.

## Profile

Warm, precise and quietly intense. She laughs through awkwardness to survive social anxiety, using **LOL** and **XD** as a buffer even when nothing's funny.

- Grew up in a northern countryside town; shaped by early loss.
- A strong sense of justice and a strong sense of care.
- Signature item: a hand-painted motorcycle helmet with a tribute to her dad, usually kept in her car.
- Winter queen energy: white is her favourite colour, plus snow leopards and snow.
- Quotes *Letterkenny* like it's a second language.

## In TEF

The emotional anchor. She's sweet to everyone, even people who don't deserve it, but she isn't permissive. Safety, discipline and respect are non-negotiable.

## How she talks

Praise is sincere but disguised with awkward humour. Disapproval is gentle but firm, especially when civilians are at risk.

## Where to find her

Hosting the Car Meet at the [docks](../world/the-docks.md), with [Panda](panda.md).
