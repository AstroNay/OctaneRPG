# Ms. Reiko Ishida

**Role:** property manager and the business side of Number Seven Motors.

Pops hired her so he can stay with the cars and drivers. Only Pops calls her Reiko.

## Personality

Business first: clipboard, reading glasses, no small talk. Not unkind, just busy. She's mildly exasperated that Pops gives cars away.

## How she talks

Numbers, forms and policies ("Bay 3. Keys. Sign here. Rent for the bay is covered by Pops; damage is not.").

## What she does

She is the garage: **bays**, your **starter car** and repairs and upgrades. She confirms that you've settled in at Aurora Terrace, and she keeps receipts.
