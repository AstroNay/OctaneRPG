# Old Mag

**Role:** junkyard and scrapyard owner.

She knows every wreck in the yard by its story, and she trades gossip for parts. She knows OTP, and she's neutral to everyone else. Pops warns that she'll still overcharge you.

## What she does

- Runs the **junkyard**. Your first search is on the house; after that you buy a pass.
- Buys your junk, spares and gear at the **scrapyard**.

## How she talks

Dry and unsentimental. "Everything in this yard has a past. Cars too."
