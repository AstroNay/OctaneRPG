# What is OctaneRPG?

OctaneRPG is a car-culture RPG you play on your PC. You live in **Aurora Hills**, a hillside city that is quiet by day and loud on weekend nights, and you work your way up from a borrowed beater to the top of the ladder.

It started life as a Discord bot. It is now a standalone game with a walkable town. The Discord bot is a [read-only viewer](../reference/discord-bot.md).

## The loop

1. **Earn**: work jobs at the Job Board, search the junkyard, scrap what you don't need.
2. **Upgrade**: install parts in your garage and watch the dyno sheet.
3. **Race**: the Zeroyon drag strip, Chibi GP, ranked matches and street races.
4. **Progress**: level up, clear challenges, climb the ladder.
5. **Show off**: your ID Card, titles and badges, the leaderboard, your crew.

The social side (crews, meets, leaderboards) is never required.

## How the game feels

- **Every system lives somewhere in town**, not in a menu. You walk to the garage, the Job Board, the junkyard and the strip, and interact with the building or the person behind it.
- **Races are battles.** Your car's stats and parts decide the result, and the dyno tells you what a part will do before you bolt it on.
- **The server is the referee.** Prices, odds and outcomes are decided online, so you need an internet connection and your progress follows your account.
