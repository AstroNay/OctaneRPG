# Accounts and Founders

## Guest accounts

Everyone starts as a **guest**. The main menu shows **New Game** when you have no saved session and **Continue** when you do. A guest account is tied to the machine it was created on until you link it.

## Linking Discord or Google

Open **Esc → Settings → Account → Link Discord** (or Google). Linking:

- ties your progress to you instead of one PC,
- keeps your **Alpha Racer** badge through alpha wipes,
- earns **Founder** rewards if you played the Discord-era game.

## Usernames

Usernames are unique and case-insensitive. If a name is taken you'll be asked to pick another.

## Founders and badges

| Badge | Who gets it |
|---|---|
| **Founder** | Players of the original Discord game who link the same Discord account |
| **Alpha Racer** | Alpha testers with a linked Discord account |
| **Beta Racer** | Beta testers (planned) |

Titles and badges are shown on your ID Card under **TITLES**. Reputation ("Street Cred") and infamy are tracked alongside them.

## Wipes

The alpha can be wiped when the team needs to. Wipes are announced in Discord first and become rarer in beta. Progress becomes permanent in Early Access.
