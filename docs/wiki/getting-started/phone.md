# The Moshi Mobile phone

Press **Tab** to open your phone from anywhere. It works while you walk and drive; you just can't use the interact key or the pause menu while it is open.

## Apps

| App | What it does |
|---|---|
| **ID Card** | Your public card (CARD tab) and your stats (STATS tab). Titles and badges are here too. |
| **Messages** | Story texts, rewards, crew invites and system notices, grouped into threads per sender. |
| **Rewards** | Daily and weekly claims. Daily resets at midnight Eastern time. |
| **Chibi GP** | The idle racer. See [Racing](../gameplay/racing.md#chibi-gp). |
| **Crew** | Browse, join and manage crews. See [Crews](../gameplay/crews.md). |
| **Leaderboard** | Podium, rankings and your own pinned row. |

## Messages

- Threads are named after the sender: Pops, Ms. Ishida, Sweets, Flauntive, your crew, and "Octane" for everything else.
- Rewards arrive as mail. **Claim** one or **claim all**. Unclaimed rewards can't be deleted.
- A badge on the status-bar chat bubble shows unread messages.
- Rewards are purged after 90 days, notifications after 30 and announcements after 180.

## ID Card privacy

Your own card shows stats but never your currencies, and other players see your public card without currencies too.
