# Your first day: Chapter 0 "Fresh Start"

Chapter 0 walks a new player from waking up in a rented apartment to their first drag race. It doubles as the tutorial. This page is spoiler-light: it tells you where you are going, not what anyone says.

## The steps

| # | Mission | What you do |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | Wake up | Read your phone, reply to Pops, choose the name you'll go by in Aurora Hills |
| 1 | Come by the garage | Meet [Pops](../characters/pops.md) at Number Seven Motors |
| 2 | Bay 3 | [Ms. Ishida](../characters/ms-ishida.md) hands you the keys; pick your starter car |
| 3 | Rent doesn't pay itself | Work one job at the Job Board |
| 4 | More in the tank | Do one junkyard search; [Old Mag](../characters/old-mag.md) runs the yard |
| 5 | Bolt it on | Install a part and check the dyno sheet before and after |
| 6 | Friday night at Zeroyon | Finish your first drag race against [Jun "Jumpstart" Park](../characters/jun-park.md) |
| 7 | Word gets around | Read the texts that come in once people notice you |

You don't have to do these in order. If you work a job before visiting the garage, the matching text simply arrives when its step is done. After mission 2, nothing blocks free play.

## Tips

- **Press Tab** to open your phone. Pops texts you the next step, so check Messages when you're not sure what to do.
- **Wait for green** at the drag strip. Leaving early is a red light and you lose, however fast your car is. See [Racing](../gameplay/racing.md).
- Your starter grant includes starter coins, lottery tokens and one free junkyard pass. See [Junkyard](../gameplay/junkyard-and-scrapyard.md).
- Your chosen name must be unique.

## What's next

Once Chapter 0 is done you meet both of the city's crews: [The Elite Few](../factions/the-elite-few.md) and [Own The Pavement](../factions/own-the-pavement.md). Neither will let you join, but both are watching.
