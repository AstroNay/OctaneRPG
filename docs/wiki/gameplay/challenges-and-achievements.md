# Challenges and achievements

## Challenges

Open the challenges window to see three tabs.

| Tab | What it is | Resets |
|---|---|---|
| **Daily** | A fresh set each day | Midnight Eastern |
| **Starter** | The new-player tutorial chain | Never |
| **World** | Long-term goals | Never |

Rewards arrive as Messages mail and are [boosted](../reference/currencies.md#boosters) if you have a booster active.

**Daily streak:** the Daily tab tracks your current and best streak.

**World goals** are earned by walking, driving and visiting districts, for example walking 500 and 5,000 units, driving 1,000 and 10,000, and entering districts 10 and 40 times. Daily caps apply.

## Starter chain

Job → junkyard → install a part → first drag race. This is the same chain [Chapter 0](../getting-started/first-day.md) follows.

## Achievements

Open them from the pause menu or your ID Card. They are sorted into in-progress (by percent), locked, then completed (newest first). Completing one shows a toast.
