# Junkyard and scrapyard

The south-side **junkyard** is where parts come from. [Old Mag](../characters/old-mag.md) runs it and knows every wreck by its story.

## Searching

- A search costs one **Junkyard Pass**. New players get one free; more come from the store.
- A full bag never stops you: extra loot goes to your **overflow container** at the junkyard storage.
- Typical results: most finds are junk (**Worn**, **Broken**, **Damaged**) and about 1 in 20 is a **Usable** part you can install.

| Condition | Rarity | Value |
|---|---|---|
| Worn | Common | 5% of part value |
| Broken | Common | 10% |
| Damaged | Uncommon | 25% |
| Usable | Rare | Installable |

Expect roughly 43 coins per part and about 200 coins per search in value.

## Scrapyard

Sell junk, spares and unwanted gear to Old Mag.

- Junk pays its value × quantity.
- Usable parts pay 50%.
- Gear pays 10 / 25 / 75 / 200 coins by rarity.
- **Equipped gear can't be sold.**
- If your bag changed since you opened the window, the sale is refused and you try again.
- **Select all junk** and **Select all gear** speed up cleaning out your bag. Gear selection keeps anything better than what you're wearing.

## Storage

The junkyard storage building holds your overflow. You can take whole stacks (right-click) or one item (Shift + right-click).
