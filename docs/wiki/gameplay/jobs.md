# Jobs

The **Job Board** is where you find paid work. It is run by **Daichi "Foreman" Ota**, who judges people by whether they show up on time.

## How it works

1. **Apply** for a job.
2. **Work** it when it's ready. Each job has a cooldown shown as minutes until ready.
3. Collect coins and XP.

- Your first job is instantly workable.
- If you **switch jobs** the work cooldown carries over, so you can't job-hop to skip waiting.
- Working your first job completes a [Chapter 0](../getting-started/first-day.md) step.
- Jobs are affected by [boosters](../reference/currencies.md#boosters).
