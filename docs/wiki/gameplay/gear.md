# Gear and the vending machine

Gear is wearable cosmetics-with-a-point: head, face and body slots, four rarities (common, uncommon, rare, ultra). Equipped gear counts toward a car's [PI](performance-and-classes.md). There are 33 catalog items in the alpha.

## The vending machine

Found in the **Garage** district.

1. Spend **1 Podium Point** to roll.
2. You get **3 cards**. Keep one.
3. The pick goes to your bag, or to storage if the bag is full.

Each card rolls a slot, then a rarity close to what you already wear in that slot. About 36% of draws offer at least one upgrade. Your draw is saved, so closing the game doesn't lose it.

## Sponsor Deal

Pay coins to raise your **Sponsor Deal** (10 levels). Higher levels raise the rarity ceiling and shift the odds toward upgrades. It is upgraded from the vending machine window.

## Podium Points

You start with 5 and earn 5 each time you reach a new ladder ceiling in Chibi GP.
