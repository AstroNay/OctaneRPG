# Racing

There are four ways to race.

| Mode | Where | Unlocks | Style |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Zeroyon drag strip](#zeroyon-drag-strip) | Race district | Start | Reaction + ET over 400 m |
| [Chibi GP](#chibi-gp) | Phone app | Start | Idle ladder |
| [Ranked](#ranked) | Race menu | Level 5 | Instant, class-locked duels |
| [Street](#street) | Race menu | Level 10 | AI opponents plus police chases |

## Zeroyon drag strip

The local 400 m strip, where the locals run on Friday nights. It is the starter race and the finale of [Chapter 0](../getting-started/first-day.md).

**The start:** pre-stage, stage, three ambers, then green. Launch on green with **Space** or left mouse.

**Who wins:** the lowest *reaction time + ET* wins. Leaving before green is a **red light** and an automatic loss. Ties go to you.

**Rewards:**

| Result | Pay |
|---|---|
| Quick match win | 150 coins, 20 XP |
| Loss | 25% of the win pay |
| First time beating a ladder rival | One-time bonus, about 5× a normal win |

A run costs **10 fuel**. Your reaction is measured on your machine. Quick match pairs you with a car within ±0.4 s of yours.

**Rivals:** [Jun "Jumpstart" Park](../characters/jun-park.md) is rival 1. Others on the ladder are Sora Hasegawa, Ren Aoyama, Goro Kobayashi and Kaito Shimada. Each rival has a driving style; Jun jumps the start about one run in five.

## Chibi GP

An idle ladder you run from the phone.

- It ticks about every 10 seconds, costs no fuel and pays nothing per tick. Ticks move you up the ladder and can earn **Podium Points** when you reach a new ceiling.
- Every **5 minutes** it pays coins and XP, scaled by your level. Climbing pays 100%, repeating a level pays 10% and being offline pays 5% (up to 8 hours).
- Every **10th level is a boss**. Losing to a boss sends you back to the start of that bracket; you can challenge the boss again directly.
- Opponents scale with ladder level, not with your car's power.
- Idle races don't count toward "first race" or daily race challenges, except climbing five new levels.

## Ranked

Instant server-resolved duels against players in your class. Uses a duel rating. Unlocks at level 5.

## Street

AI opponents scaled into your class, and a police chase minigame (the same one the crime jobs use). Unlocks at level 10. See [Crime and heat](crime-and-heat.md).
