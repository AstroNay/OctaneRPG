# Performance index and classes

Every car has a **Performance Index (PI)**, a single number built from six stats across the stock car, its installed parts and any equipped gear. PI is calculated live; blessings and fuel are not included.

## Classes

| Class | Minimum PI |
|---|---|
| D | 0 |
| C | 500 |
| B | 700 |
| A | 950 |
| S | 1250 |

*Alpha values; thresholds may be tuned.*

Your car shows a class badge. Classes matter in two places:

- **Ranked** matches are strictly within your class. If nobody in your class is available, there is no match.
- **Street races** scale their AI opponents into your class.

See [Racing](racing.md).
