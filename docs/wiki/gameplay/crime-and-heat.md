# Crime and heat

The **Back Alley** (level 10) is where the money is faster and the risk is real. Respect from [Own The Pavement](../factions/own-the-pavement.md) is quick and leads back to jail; that is the story's warning, and heat is the game's version of it.

## Crimes

| Job | Notes |
|---|---|
| Junkyard Rob | |
| Employer Steal | |
| Transport Run | |
| Lay Low | Cool things down |
| Bank Heist | Three phases: Approach, Wires, Vault (pick one of three) |

A bank heist is played out in phases, then resolved as success, partial or failure.

## Heat

- The **heat flame** on your HUD fills as you do crime. It also tracks your highest heat as a career stat.
- High heat brings police attention, which leads to **chases**.
- A chase is a minigame: surrender, or escape and time the needle. The same minigame appears in street races.

## Faction effects

[TEF](../factions/the-elite-few.md) notices dirty work, and reckless chases drop their respect sharply. OTP work raises heat and OTP's attention. You can have high respect with both, at a cost.
