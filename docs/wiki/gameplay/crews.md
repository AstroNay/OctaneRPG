# Crews

A **crew** is a player-made group with a tag, a bank and a level. Manage yours from the phone's **Crew** app.

> TEF and OTP are **story crews**. You can earn their respect but you can never join them. See [TEF vs OTP](../factions/tef-vs-otp.md).

## Rules

- One crew per player.
- After you leave, are kicked or your crew disbands there is a **24-hour cooldown** before you can join another. There is also a **5-day leave lock** after joining.
- One owner. The owner can't leave without transferring or disbanding.
- Admins can kick plain members and toggle privacy.
- **Invites** arrive as Messages, expire after 48 hours, and a crew can have 20 pending.

## Progression

| | |
|---|---|
| Bank cap | 50,000 coins |
| Upgrade cost | 10,000 × level |
| Max level | 5 |
| Tag styles | Sunset, ocean, toxic, royal, chrome (unlock at level 3) |

**Donations:** up to 5,000 coins per day, converted at 10 coins per Crew Token. You earn reputation for donating.

## Banners

Pick from 12 preset banners in the crew's STYLE tab.
