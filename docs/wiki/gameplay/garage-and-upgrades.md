# Garage and upgrades

Your garage is **Number Seven Motors**, Pops's shop. [Ms. Ishida](../characters/ms-ishida.md) handles bays, keys and repairs. Your first bay is Bay 3.

## Cars

- Every car is a fictional JDM-style model; there are no real makes.
- You can drive any car you own. Press **V** to get in or out.
- Cars are bought at the dealership or found in the world.
- Each car has a **drivetrain** (FWD, RWD or AWD) and a weight, both of which feed into how it launches.

## Upgrading

Each car has one slot per **part type**, and each slot has a level from **0 to 50**. Installing a part consumes one *Usable* part from your bag and raises that slot by a level.

- You can upgrade **any car you own**, not just the active one.
- **Turbo and supercharger are mutually exclusive.**
- **Weight reduction** removes 2 kg per level.
- Only seven stats matter: grip, suspension, brakes, torque, horsepower, aero and durability.

See the [parts reference](../reference/parts.md) for all 15 part types.

## The dyno

The dyno sheet in your bay shows your car's predicted **ET** (elapsed time over 400 m) and the seconds the next install of each part would save, or why it's blocked (maxed or exclusive). It runs the same simulation as a real drag run, so the dyno matches the strip.
