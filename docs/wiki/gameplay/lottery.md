# Lucky draw (the crane machine)

A crane machine beside the Market in the junkyard district. Spend **Lucky Tokens** for a prize capsule.

- **1 to 10 draws** per visit. No cooldown.
- Prizes: coins, XP, market coupons (basic and premium), more tokens.
- Tier odds: common 55%, uncommon 20%, rare 15%, ultra 10%.
- Tokens: you get **3 each week** from the weekly reward; the stack caps at 50. You also start with a few.
- The result is decided before the claw moves; skipping the animation never changes the prize.
