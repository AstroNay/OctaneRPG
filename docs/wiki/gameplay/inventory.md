# Bag and weight

Press **I** to open your bag. It holds parts and spare gear and has a weight limit.

| | |
|---|---|
| Capacity | 36 |
| Default item weight | 5 |

## Part weights

| Weight | Parts |
|---|---|
| Heavy (12) | Engine, transmission |
| Medium (9) | Turbo, supercharger, suspension, coilovers, intercooler |
| Utility (5) | Aero, nitrous, weight reduction |
| Light (3) | Tires, wheels, brakes, intake, exhaust |

A mixed bag holds roughly 5–7 parts. **Installed parts and equipped gear weigh nothing**; spare gear weighs 1–3.

## Overflow

You never lose items to a full bag. New finds overflow to the junkyard storage container. The weight bar turns orange at 70% and red at 90%.

Bag capacity upgrades are planned.
