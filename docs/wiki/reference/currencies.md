# Currencies

The status bar shows eight currency pills with tooltips. Large balances are abbreviated (1.2K); prices never are.

| Currency | Earned from | Spent on |
|---|---|---|
| **Coins** | Jobs, races, crime, Chibi GP, scrapyard, rewards | Cars, parts, store, Sponsor Deal, fuel |
| **XP / level** | Almost everything | Unlocks features |
| **Podium Points** | +5 per Chibi GP ceiling advance; start with 5 | Gear draws (1 each) |
| **Crew Tokens** | Rewards, activities | Boosters, crew donations |
| **Lucky Tokens** | Weekly reward (3) | [Lucky draw](../gameplay/lottery.md); max stack 50 |
| **Junkyard Passes** | One free to start, then store | [Junkyard searches](../gameplay/junkyard-and-scrapyard.md) |
| **Coupons** | Lottery, rewards | Market supply runs |
| **Feast supplies** | Lottery, rewards | Market supply runs |

## Boosters

- **Coin booster** and **XP booster**: 400 Crew Tokens each, last 24 hours, don't stack.
- **Boosted:** jobs, drag races (including first-beat bonus), crime, daily and weekly claims, challenge rewards, story XP.
- **Not boosted:** Chibi GP, mailbox claims, scrapyard, lottery, car meets, ranked, street races, refunds.

## Rewards

Daily and weekly claims are in the phone's Rewards app. Daily resets at midnight Eastern.

## Market

The market's supply-run slots unlock at levels 1, 5, 8, 10, 12 and 15. Premium runs yield 5, basic yield 1.

## Shrine

Unlocks at level 5. Roll for a blessing and lock or unlock it. Gated by cost, not time.
