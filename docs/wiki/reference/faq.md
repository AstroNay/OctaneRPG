# FAQ

**Is OctaneRPG free?** The closed alpha is free.

**What platform?** Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit.

**Do I need an internet connection?** Yes. Your progress is saved online.

**Can I join TEF or OTP?** No. They're story crews. You earn respect, not membership. See [TEF vs OTP](../factions/tef-vs-otp.md).

**I played the Discord version. Do I keep anything?** The new game starts from zero. Link your Discord account to earn Founder rewards. See [Accounts and Founders](../getting-started/accounts-and-founders.md).

**Will the alpha be wiped?** It might. Wipes are announced in Discord first.

**I lost a race even though my car is faster.** Check for a red light, then look at your reaction time. Lowest reaction + ET wins.

**My bag is full. Did I lose items?** No. New finds overflow to junkyard storage.

**I found a bug.** Press **Esc → Report a bug**.

**Where do I give feedback?** The `#feedback` channel in [Discord](https://discord.octanerpg.com/).
