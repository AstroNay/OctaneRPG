# The docks

The docks are where Aurora Hills began and where drifting lives now. They were once a sprawling economy of their own, with fish markets that drew a large population and the road network that still carries the city's traffic.

## What's there

Stacks of shipping containers, steel structures, a rusting docked ship, and the old roads that once moved imports and exports.

## Who's there

Fishermen cast off the edge by day. The area isn't fenced or private, and police rarely patrol it, especially at night.

## Why TEF chose it

Businesses don't want their lots filled by drivers who aren't customers, and "takeover" crews spoil lot meets with burnouts and drifting. [TEF](../factions/the-elite-few.md) looks down on takeovers and moved to the docks.

To TEF it is the safest place to drift outside a track. The barriers are steel and containers, so when accidents happen the risk falls on the driver, not an owner who left long ago. Their regular presence also keeps [OTP](../factions/own-the-pavement.md) from using the docks for worse things.

## In game

The docks are the **Harbor** district and are not yet reachable. The Car Meet will be hosted here. See [Districts](districts.md).
