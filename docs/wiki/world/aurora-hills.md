# Aurora Hills

A hillside city with a big car culture: quiet by day, loud on weekend nights. Its names lean Japanese-flavoured but stay easy to read.

## History

Aurora Hills grew out of its **docks**. Fishing exports, fish markets and the road network built to haul them made the city, and the city outlived them. As export demand slowed the docks were left behind as a remnant of richer times. The roads stayed, and so did the car culture.

## Culture

Two crews define the city's scene: the quiet, selective [Elite Few](../factions/the-elite-few.md) and the loud, open [Own The Pavement](../factions/own-the-pavement.md). Between them are the garages, the strip regulars and the people who just like cars.
