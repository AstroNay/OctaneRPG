# OctaneRPG Wiki

**OctaneRPG** is a retro, pixel-art racing RPG set in the hillside city of **Aurora Hills**. You walk and drive around a 2D town, take jobs, dig through the junkyard, tune your cars and race for respect. Races are battles, cars are your party and horsepower is your attack stat.

> **Alpha notice:** the game is in closed alpha. Numbers, prices and unlock levels on this wiki are the current alpha values and can change between builds. Pages marked *planned* describe things that are not playable yet.
